# Huong dan lenh dac biet

Tai lieu nay mo ta cac lenh dac biet trong F.R.I.D.A.Y va cach su dung nhanh.

## Tong quan

- Lenh bat dau bang dau "/" (vi du: /help).
- Goi lenh truc tiep trong hoi thoai de thuc thi.

## Danh sach lenh

### /help
Hien thi tom tat tat ca lenh dac biet va huong dan su dung.

### /bye | /exit | /quit
Thoat phien lam viec va dung chuong trinh.

### /clear
Xoa lich su hoi thoai da luu trong bo nho.

### /check-files
Liet ke cac file dang co trong vault (thu muc data).

### /search <tu_khoa>
Dieu huong tim thong tin tren mang (chua can setup, se cap nhat sau).

### /status
Hien thi trang thai he thong: Boss, mode, so lich su, so TODO, duong dan data.

### /voice on|off|status|help
Bat/tat voice listener va xem trang thai.

Vi du:
- /voice on
- /voice status
- /voice off

### /mode <ten>
Xem hoac doi che do lam viec.

Vi du:
- /mode think
- /mode default

### /todo [list|add <noi_dung>]
Quan ly danh sach viec can lam.

Vi du:
- /todo
- /todo list
- /todo add mua sua

Vi du /search:
- /search xu huong AI 2026

## Luu y

- Lenh khong phan biet hoa thuong.
- Neu go sai cu phap, he thong se nhac lai cach dung.
